Full-screen breaks in the app

In the free Cable TV app, a short break comes up on full-screen channels and films, at most every 3 minutes. Your ad takes its turn there.

Ad lengths and rules

  • Video ads can be 5, 10, 15, 20 … up to 60 seconds (any multiple of 5). A picture ad shows for 20 seconds.
  • A break is never longer than 60 seconds in all, so your ad is never lost in a long block.
  • Viewers can skip an ad after 10 seconds; ads of 10 seconds or less always play to the end.
  • Pressing OK on your ad opens your website, right inside the app.
  • Family-friendly ads only. We check every ad before it goes on air.

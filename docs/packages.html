What's in each package

Each package has everything in the one before it, plus more. The prices shown are the ones in Settings above.

Free $0

For: anyone who just wants to watch one channel at a time.

  • All channels, full screen, favourites, channel numbers and search
  • Browse: the home screen with rows of big channel cards
  • 1+List: one channel with the channel list beside it
  • Carousel: one big channel, slide left and right to the next
  • Strip: a big channel on top, small channel tiles along the bottom

Silver

For: people who like to keep an eye on a second channel.

  • Everything in Free, plus:
  • 1×2: two channels side by side
  • 1+3: one big channel and three small ones beside it
  • Duo: two channels playing on top, channel cards below

Gold

For: families and news watchers who want the TV to show more than one thing.

  • Everything in Silver, plus:
  • 2×2: four channels at once
  • News: your channel with weather, prayer times, traffic cameras and headlines around it
  • CP24: a big channel with a clock, weather and scrolling lines, like the Toronto news channel
  • Home: your channel with cards for the clock, weather, prayers, stories and markets
  • My Screen: a screen the viewer designs themselves
  • Movies & Dramas: the Library of movies and drama series

Platinum

For: the biggest fans, or a home with two TVs.

  • Everything in Gold, plus:
  • 2×3: six channels at once
  • Games: the 12 retro games played with the TV remote
  • 2 devices: one account works on two TVs or phones at the same time (every other package works on one)

How it works

  • Packages off (the switch in Settings is unticked): every viewer has everything, like Platinum, for free. Nothing is locked.
  • Packages on: a viewer without a package has Free. Locked modes show a 🔒 and the package they need; picking one opens the packages screen in the app.
  • Free trial: a brand-new viewer gets everything (Platinum) for the trial days in Settings, counted from the day they first signed in. When the trial ends they drop to Free, unless you gave them a package. This is different from the Free package, which only has the basic modes and never ends.
  • Buying: in the app the viewer picks a package and a length and presses Ask. That sends you a message in ✉ Messages. You reply with how to pay, and once they've paid you give them the package below.
  • Ending: 5 days before a package (or the trial) ends, the viewer sees a reminder once a day with Renew and Message us buttons. When it ends they drop to Free, and for a week the app tells them it ended.
  • Renewing: giving the same package again adds the time after the current end date, so paying early loses nothing. Giving a different package starts it today.

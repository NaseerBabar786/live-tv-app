Sponsors

Businesses that pay to be shown in Free Live TV (version 1.8.90 and newer). Each one shows in turn on the start screen, in the strip under the 1+List channel list, in a bar under the tiles of 1×2, 2×2 and 2×3 (their wide banner if you add one), under the side tiles of 1+3, in the corner of News mode (as a muted video when you add a video link), and in a small corner card after a channel change (at most every 20 minutes). How often each was shown is on the Stats page. TVs pick up changes the next time the app starts.

Scrolling line

A line of text that scrolls across once every 30 seconds (Free Live TV 1.8.96 and newer): bigger in the band above the players in 1×2 and 2×2, and next to the channel count in the other layouts. It never runs over a channel being watched full screen.

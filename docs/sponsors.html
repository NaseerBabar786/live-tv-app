Sponsors

Businesses that pay to be shown in Cable TV (version 1.8.90 and newer). Each one shows in turn in the strip under the 1+List channel list, in a bar under the tiles of 1×2, 2×2 and 2×3 (their wide banner if you add one), under the side tiles of 1+3, in the corner of News mode (as a muted video when you add a video link), and in a pop-up ad break on full-screen channels (at most every 3 minutes, up to 2 ads back to back, never more than 1 minute in all; viewers can skip each ad after 5 seconds): their picture, or, if you choose Video, their video (at most every 30 minutes). Video ads must be 5, 10, 15, 20 … up to 60 seconds long (any multiple of 5); this page measures the video and warns you when it isn't, with a ✏️ Fix button that opens the ad. OK on any ad opens their website inside the app. How often each was shown is on the Stats page. TVs pick up changes the next time the app starts.

This page will work once Firebase is set up.
This account isn't an admin.

Scrolling line

A line of text that scrolls across once every 30 seconds (Cable TV 1.8.96 and newer): bigger in the band above the players in 1×2 and 2×2, and next to the channel count in the other layouts, and along the bottom of a full-screen channel and News mode. From Cable TV 1.11.0 it takes turns with a line about our packages (Free and Gold).

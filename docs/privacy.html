Privacy policy

Free Live TV is a free app for Android phones and TVs, made by Bulk Bazaar Inc. This page explains what we collect and why. Last updated 6 October 2026.

What we collect

To use Free Live TV you sign in with an email and password account you make in the app, or with your Google account. When you do, we keep:

  • your name and email address (the ones you typed, or from your Google account),
  • your password, stored scrambled by Google Firebase: nobody, not even us, can read it. If you forget it, we can set a new one for you,
  • your package (Free, Silver, Gold or Platinum) and when it ends, if you buy one,
  • when you first signed in and when you last opened the app,
  • the app version, the type of device (TV or phone) and its model,
  • a random device code made by the app, so an account can be used on one device at a time (two with Platinum),
  • which channels you watch and for how many minutes each day (from version 1.8.88), so we can see what viewers like most,
  • how many times each sponsor's ad was shown on your device each day (from version 1.8.90), so we can tell sponsors how often their ad was seen (only totals; sponsors never see who you are),
  • anything you post in Suggestions, with your name and the time, and your private messages with the Free Live TV team.

On this website

When you visit tv.bulkbazaar.ca, your browser gets a random, anonymous ID (no name or email). Each day we count which pages it opened, whether it was a computer or phone, and which channels it watched here and for how many minutes. We use these totals only to see how many people use the website.

We don't collect your contacts or location. The weather and prayer times use a city you type in Settings, or, if you allow it, your device's approximate location, or else your approximate location from your internet address. The location stays on your device; only the map position is sent to the free weather and prayer-time services to look them up, and it isn't stored.

Why

  • To know how many people use Free Live TV, on which devices, and which channels are watched most, so we can improve it.
  • To keep each free account to one device at a time.
  • To let app users post and reply in Suggestions, under their name.

Who can see it

Only the Free Live TV team can see the user list and viewing totals. Your name and posts in Suggestions are visible to other Free Live TV users. Private messages are seen only by you and the Free Live TV team. We don't sell or share your information with anyone else.

The data is stored with Google Firebase (Google LLC), which handles sign-in and storage for us. See Firebase's privacy information.

Removing your data

You can sign out at any time in the app's Settings. To have your account and posts deleted, contact us through www.bulkbazaar.ca from the email address you signed in with, and we'll remove them.

Children

Free Live TV isn't meant for children under 13 to sign in to on their own. A parent should sign in for them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.

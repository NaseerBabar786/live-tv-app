Privacy policy

Live TV is a free app for Android phones and TVs, made by Bulk Bazaar Inc. This page explains what we collect and why. Last updated 3 October 2026.

What we collect

To use Live TV you sign in with your Google account. When you do, we keep:

  • your name and email address from your Google account,
  • when you first signed in and when you last opened the app,
  • the app version, the type of device (TV or phone) and its model,
  • a random device code made by the app, so a free account can be used on one device at a time,
  • which channels you watch and for how many minutes each day (from version 1.8.88), so we can see what viewers like most,
  • anything you post in Suggestions, with your name and the time.

We don't collect your password, contacts or location. The weather at the top of the screen uses your approximate location from your internet address; it isn't stored.

Why

  • To know how many people use Live TV, on which devices, and which channels are watched most, so we can improve it.
  • To keep each free account to one device at a time.
  • To let app users post and reply in Suggestions, under their name.

Who can see it

Only the Live TV team can see the user list and viewing totals. Your name and posts in Suggestions are visible to other Live TV users. We don't sell or share your information with anyone else.

The data is stored with Google Firebase (Google LLC), which handles sign-in and storage for us. See Firebase's privacy information.

Removing your data

You can sign out at any time in the app's Settings. To have your account and posts deleted, contact us through www.bulkbazaar.ca from the email address you signed in with, and we'll remove them.

Children

Live TV isn't meant for children under 13 to sign in to on their own. A parent should sign in for them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.

Advertise on Cable TV

Cable TV is a free app that families use to watch Pakistani, Indian, Canadian, British and American channels, mostly on the big TV in the living room. Your business can be part of it.

Who watches

Mostly Pakistani and Indian families in Canada, watching news, dramas and sports in Urdu, Hindi, Punjabi and English. Every viewer signs in, so we count real people, not clicks.

Where your ad shows

Beside the channel listYour picture under the channel list on the main TV screen, in turn with other sponsors.
Under two channelsA bar with your picture and phone number under the two channels of the 1×2 layout.
Channel change cardA small card in the corner after a channel change, now and then. It never covers the show.

There are no pop-ups, no sound and no video ads. Ads never interrupt a show.

What you get

  • A simple monthly report: how many times your ad was shown and on how many TVs and phones.
  • Change your picture any time, for example for Eid, Diwali or a weekend sale.
  • Choose your dates: one month, three months, or longer.

How to start

  1. Email us your business name, phone number or website, and a wide picture (16:9, for example 1280 × 720).
  2. We'll reply with prices and the dates available.
  3. Your ad goes live in the app the same day.

How it works (read this first)
  1. Videos (step 2): add each video with a direct link to an MP4 file. The length fills in by itself.
  2. Non-stop loop (step 3): the order your videos play round the clock, like a playlist on repeat. Put ads between programmes here.

2 Videos

Where do I put my videos? (important)

The free plan can't store video files, so each video needs a direct link to an MP4 file: a link that, opened in a browser, shows just the video.

  • archive.org (free, no limit): sign in, Upload, wait for it to finish, open the item, under "Download options" right-click MPEG4 › Copy link. The link looks like https://archive.org/download/…/….mp4
  • Dropbox: Share › Copy link. Paste it here; the page changes the ending to raw=1 for you.
  • Live streams (.m3u8): also fine. Leave the length empty; they play until the next time slot.
  • Not supported: YouTube, Facebook and Google Drive links. Their players won't run inside TV apps.
  • Best format: MP4, H.264 video, AAC sound, 720p or 1080p.

3 Non-stop loop

Plays from top to bottom, then starts again, all day and night. A video can be in the loop more than once (handy for ads). Total: 0:00

How the loop works
  • Use ▲ ▼ to change the order and ✕ to take a video out of the loop (it stays in your Videos list).
  • The loop is timed by the clock, so all viewers are at the same place in it.
  • If the loop is empty and no time slot is on, the channel shows "off air" with what's next.

4 Time slots (optional)

How time slots work
  • A slot starts its video at the exact time, on the days you choose, in the time zone from step 1.
  • When the video ends, the loop carries on. If the next slot starts first, it cuts the video short.
  • A slot for a date (e.g. Eid) wins over an "every day" slot at the same time.

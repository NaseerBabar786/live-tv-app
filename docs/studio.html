  1. 📚 Library: every programme our daily search finds on this channel's official YouTube sources. Watch any with ▶ Preview. Nothing ever leaves the Library.
  2. ✅ Next transmission: the programmes you approve from the Library. Only these go on air. Press 💾 Save programmes after changes.

Where do I put my videos? (important)

The free plan can't store video files, so each video needs a direct link to an MP4 file: a link that, opened in a browser, shows just the video.

  • archive.org (free, no limit): sign in, Upload, wait for it to finish, open the item, under "Download options" right-click MPEG4 › Copy link. The link looks like https://archive.org/download/…/….mp4
  • Dropbox: Share › Copy link. Paste it here; the page changes the ending to raw=1 for you.
  • Live streams (.m3u8): also fine. Leave the length empty; they play until the next time slot.
  • Not supported: YouTube, Facebook and Google Drive links. Their players won't run inside TV apps.
  • Best format: MP4, H.264 video, AAC sound, 720p or 1080p.

How the loop works
  • Use ▲ ▼ to change the order and ✕ Remove to take a video out of the loop (it stays in your Videos list).
  • The row with ▶ ON TV NOW is what viewers are watching at this moment.
  • The loop is timed by the clock, so all viewers are at the same place in it.
  • If the loop is empty and no time slot is on, the channel shows "off air" with what's next.

4 Time slots (optional)

How time slots work
  • A slot starts its video at the exact time, on the days you choose, in the time zone from step 1.
  • When the video ends, the loop carries on. If the next slot starts first, it cuts the video short.
  • A slot for a date (e.g. Eid) wins over an "every day" slot at the same time.

📅 Weekly schedule

Your week at a glance, in Toronto time. Blocks are booked shows (time slots); purple ones are weekly shows with episodes (a drama or serial: a new episode each time). The rest of the time the non-stop loop plays. Click an empty hour to book a show there; click a block to change or remove it.

🎬 Coming-up promos for your shows

Every booked show gets its own 20-second promo, like real channels run: quick cuts of the show itself, its name and time, an announcer and our own music. Promos are made by themselves within about an hour after you Save a booking. Add them to the loop and they play in the breaks (each break stays at 60 seconds or less).

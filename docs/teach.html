Teach from home

Teach Quran, Qaida, Tajweed, Hifz, Urdu or Arabic online to Cable TV and Spark TV families. Joining is free, and you only pay when you teach.

How it works

  1. Fill in the form below. We check every teacher, usually within a day, then you appear on the lessons page.
  2. A family books on WhatsApp. We send you their name and phone number.
  3. You teach on WhatsApp video or Zoom, at times you agree together, and the family pays you.
  4. Each month you send us 10% of what you earned from these families by Interac e-Transfer. You keep the other 90%.

About you

Only we see your phone number and city; families book through us.

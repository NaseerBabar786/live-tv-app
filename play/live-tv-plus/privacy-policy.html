Live TV Plus privacy policy

Last updated: October 2, 2026

Live TV Plus is a video player for Android phones, tablets and Android TV. It plays playlists (M3U links or files) that you add yourself. The app does not include, sell or recommend any channels, playlists or video content.

Information we collect

None. Live TV Plus has no accounts, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. We do not collect, store, sell or share any personal information.

Premium subscription

Premium is sold and billed by Google Play. Your payment details go to Google, never to us. The app only asks Google Play whether your Google account has an active Premium subscription, and remembers the answer on your device.

Weather

The top bar shows the local temperature. To find your area, the app asks GeoJS (geojs.io) for the approximate city of your internet connection's IP address, then asks Open-Meteo (open-meteo.com) for the weather there. The app does not use your device's GPS or location permission, and neither answer is stored or sent to us. GeoJS and Open-Meteo see your IP address as part of the request, and their own privacy policies apply.

What stays on your device

  • The playlists you add (their names and links, or access to the files you open).
  • Your favourite channels and your last watched channel.

These settings are saved only on your device. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes them.

Network use

To play a channel, the app connects directly to the playlist and stream addresses you added, and loads channel logos listed in your playlist. Those servers are run by third parties, not by us, and their own privacy policies apply. We never receive this traffic.

Children

Live TV Plus is not directed at children under 13 and collects no information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

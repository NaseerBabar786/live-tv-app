Stream Player Plus privacy policy

Last updated: September 29, 2026

Stream Player Plus is a video player for Android phones, tablets and Android TV. It plays playlists (M3U links or files) that you add yourself. The app does not include, sell or recommend any channels, playlists or video content.

Information we collect

None. Stream Player Plus has no accounts, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. We do not collect, store, sell or share any personal information.

What stays on your device

  • The playlists you add (their names and links, or access to the files you open).
  • Your favourite channels and your last watched channel.

These settings are saved only on your device. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes them.

Network use

To play a channel, the app connects directly to the playlist and stream addresses you added, and loads channel logos listed in your playlist. Those servers are run by third parties, not by us, and their own privacy policies apply. We never receive this traffic.

Children

Stream Player Plus is not directed at children under 13 and collects no information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

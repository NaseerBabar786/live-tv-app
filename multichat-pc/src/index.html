Welcome to Multi Chat

Use several WhatsApp accounts on this PC at the same time, each in its own separate box.

  1. Press Add account and give it a name and colour, like “Bulk Bazaar” or “Personal”.
  2. On your phone, open WhatsApp, tap ⋮ > Linked devices > Link a device, and scan the QR code.
  3. Repeat for each account. Every box stays signed in on its own.

Multi Chat opens the official WhatsApp Web for each account. It is not made by or connected with WhatsApp or Meta.

Quick replies

Click a reply to type it into the open chat. It is never sent by itself: you press Send.
